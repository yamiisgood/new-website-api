HOW THE MINIGAME WORKS

01

LOAD

The frontend requests all characters from your existing FastAPI.

02

RANDOMIZE

JavaScript randomly chooses one character and creates the selected clue.

03

GUESS

You get three attempts. Wrong answers add blood effects and remove a life.

04

REVEAL

After a correct guess or three misses, the full character is revealed.
